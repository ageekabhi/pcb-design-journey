# Day 2: LED and Current-Limiting Resistor

## Objective
Understand why an LED needs a series resistor.

## Components
- 5 V DC supply
- Red LED (assumed 2 V forward voltage)
- 620 ohm resistor

## Calculation
Assumed LED current = 5 mA

R = (5 - 2) / 0.005
R = 600 ohms

Selected standard resistor = 620 ohms

Approximate current = (5 - 2) / 620
                    = 4.84 mA

## What I Learned
- A resistor limits current.
- An LED has polarity.
- Ohm's law helps calculate resistance.
- Circuit simulators let me test designs without physical hardware.
