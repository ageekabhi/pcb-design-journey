# Day 1: Ohm's Law

## 1. Voltage
Voltage is the electrical potential difference between two points.
Unit: volt (V).

## 2. Current
Current is the rate of flow of electric charge.
Unit: ampere (A).

## 3. Resistance
Resistance opposes the flow of electric current.
Unit: ohm (Ω).

## 4. Ohm's Law
V = I × R

I = V / R

R = V / I

## 5. Example
Supply voltage = 5 V
Resistance = 1000 ohms

Current = Voltage / Resistance
Current = 5 / 1000
Current = 0.005 A = 5 mA

## What I Learned
Ohm's law helps calculate current, voltage, and resistance
when designing electronic circuits.
